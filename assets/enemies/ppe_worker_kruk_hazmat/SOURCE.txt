Source asset: Hazmat guy 2d animations
Author: Kruk2024
Page: https://kruk2024.itch.io/hazmat-guy-2d-animations

Project use:
- Uses the E-facing idle and walk GIF animations as the base hazmat suit enemy.
- Source GIF frames are 128x128 with 8-direction isometric movement.
- Converted into 192x192 Canvas sprite strips for idle, walk, punch, hurt, and knockout.
- Suit color was manually remapped from yellow/olive hazmat to white PPE-style protection.
- Punch, hurt, and knockout strips are derived from the original idle/walk frames because the source pack only includes idle and walk.
